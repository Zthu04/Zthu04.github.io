Research Vision

My research focuses on improving the practicality of aerodynamic and structural aircraft design through Scientific Machine Learning. I aim to provide effective solutions to large-scale optimization problems characterized by massive design points and uncertain variables.

I integrate Deep Generative Models (VAE) and Probabilistic Surrogates (MDN, BNN) into multidisciplinary aircraft design environments to accelerate conceptual sizing and enhance reliability[cite: 144, 146].

Core Objectives:

  • Dimensionality reduction in complex shape optimization[cite: 144].
  • Design under uncertainty using Reliability-Based (RBDO) and Possibility-Based (PBDO) methods[cite: 147].
  • Performance sizing for Hybrid-Electric and Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) aircraft[cite: 149].

My Research

CADE: Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment

As the lead developer, I built the CADE framework to support the entire aerospace design workflow—from early-stage geometry generation to high-fidelity multidisciplinary optimization.

Hybrid-Electric Sizing & Performance

My research includes the development of sizing programs for Fuel Cell-Battery hybrid AAM aircraft and hydrogen-powered eVTOL UAVs[cite: 101, 103, 130]. I focus on balancing power/energy needs and technological constraints[cite: 30].

Operational Management & Digital Twins

I have developed optimization models for multi-vehicle point-to-point network operations, considering passenger priority and real-time battery levels for UAM Digital Twins[cite: 50, 126, 166].
