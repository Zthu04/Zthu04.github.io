About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Aerospace and Mobility Engineering at Konkuk University, Seoul[cite: 11, 13]. My research specializes in bridging the gap between advanced Generative AI and practical Aerospace Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO)[cite: 143, 148].

My primary focus is on solving the "curse of dimensionality" in aircraft design through Scientific Machine Learning[cite: 146]. By utilizing Deep Generative Models like Variational Autoencoders (VAE) and Probabilistic Surrogates such as Mixture Density Networks (MDN), I develop frameworks that allow for large-scale optimization under significant uncertainty[cite: 143, 151].

As the lead developer of the CADE environment, I have created a unified platform that integrates geometry generation, aerodynamics, and performance sizing for next-generation Hybrid-Electric and Regional Air Mobility (RAM) aircraft[cite: 119, 144].

My Research

1. Dimensionality Reduction & Generative Design

Traditional parameterization methods often require hundreds of variables, making optimization computationally expensive[cite: 143]. My research reformulates the design space using Generative AI (VAE), reducing dimensionality to a manageable set while maintaining high design effectiveness[cite: 146, 154].

Conceptual framework of VAE-based design space reduction for subsonic airfoils.

2. Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment (CADE)

I am the lead developer for CADE, a multidisciplinary framework designed to handle the entire aircraft design lifecycle. It seamlessly integrates aerodynamic evaluation with electric propulsion system sizing to support the development of Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) vehicles[cite: 119, 162].

Integrated MDO architecture of the CADE framework for hybrid-electric aircraft sizing.

3. Design Under Uncertainty (PBDO / RBDO)

I apply Possibility-Based (PBDO) and Reliability-Based Design Optimization (RBDO) to ensure that new aircraft configurations remain robust against technological and operational uncertainties, particularly in hybrid propulsion systems[cite: 147, 155].

Uncertainty quantification in hybrid-electric sizing using probabilistic surrogate modeling.
