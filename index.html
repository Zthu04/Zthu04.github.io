About Me

I am a Research Professor in Aerospace and Mobility Engineering at Konkuk University, Seoul. What drives me is not only solving today’s design problems, but building methods that expand what engineers can create—especially when systems are complex, data is imperfect, and decisions must be made under uncertainty.

My research combines Scientific Machine Learning, generative design, and probabilistic modeling to make large-scale optimization practical. I use VAE-based latent representations to simplify high-dimensional geometry design, and probabilistic surrogates (MDN, BNN) to predict performance with uncertainty awareness—so designs can be evaluated not just by “best case,” but by reliability.

As the lead developer of the Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment (CADE), I build an end-to-end workflow for conceptual sizing and performance analysis across Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) configurations and multi-energy systems (battery, hydrogen fuel cell, ICE, and hybrid architectures). My long-term vision is to create design technology that becomes a true partner in decision-making—supporting experts, accelerating innovation, and enabling safer and more sustainable mobility.

I am open to new opportunities (research, engineering, or interdisciplinary roles) where I can contribute to impactful projects and collaborate with excellent teams. I care deeply about building tools and knowledge that serve the community and ultimately contribute to human well-being.

My Research

1. Dimensionality Reduction in Design Spaces

My work reformulates feasible design domains using Generative AI. By training Variational Autoencoders on thousands of airfoil and wing shapes, we can compress hundreds of geometric variables into a latent space of just 10-20 variables without losing performance accuracy.

Coming soon

2. CADE: Multidisciplinary Design Optimization

I developed the CADE framework to bridge the gap between propulsion, aerodynamics, and structural weight. This is currently used for the concept development of Regional Air Mobility (RAM), Urban Air Mobility (UAM) and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV) aircrafts.

Coming soon

2. VAST: Versatile Aircraft Sizing Tool

I developed the VAST sizing tool to cover varsatile aircraft such as multirotor, RAM, UAM with different energy systems such Battery, Fuel cell, ICE and Hybrid. This sizing tool has been used in industry (Hyundai Motor Company) and LiG-Nex1 project.
