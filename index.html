Research vision

My research aims to improve the practicality of aircraft design through the integration of Generative AI and Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO)[cite: 5, 10, 162]. I focus on addressing challenges in high-dimensional design spaces and uncertainty quantification for next-generation mobility[cite: 51, 162].

  • Developing fast surrogate models (MDN, BNN) for on-design and off-design performance[cite: 5, 8].
  • Proposed deep generative approaches for subsonic airfoil and aircraft configuration design[cite: 16, 79].
  • Integrating reliability and possibility-based sizing for hybrid-electric aircraft[cite: 9, 17, 64].

My Research

1. AI-Enhanced Design Environments

As the lead developer of CADE (Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment), I am building a framework that supports the entire workflow from early-stage generation to multidisciplinary optimization[cite: 6, 23, 24]. It integrates geometry, aerodynamics, and propulsion for AAM aircraft[cite: 25, 106, 121].

2. Design Under Uncertainty

I specialize in Reliability-Based Design Optimization (RBDO) and Possibility-Based Design Optimization (PBDO) to ensure safety and performance in hybrid-electric systems[cite: 9, 111, 112].
