About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Aerospace and Mobility Engineering at Konkuk University. My expertise lies at the intersection of Generative AI and Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO).

My work focuses on developing AI-driven environments (like CADE) to automate and optimize the conceptual design of complex systems, specifically hybrid-electric aircraft and Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) vehicles.
