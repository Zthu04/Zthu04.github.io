01 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Better methods.
Greater possibilities.

Complex systems. Imperfect data. Decisions under uncertainty. These are the challenges that drive my work.

My research combines scientific machine learning, generative design, and probabilistic modeling to make large-scale optimization practical. I use variational autoencoder (VAE) latent representations to simplify high-dimensional geometry, and probabilistic surrogates—including mixture density networks (MDNs) and Bayesian neural networks (BNNs)—to evaluate performance and reliability.

As lead developer of the Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment (CADE), I build workflows for conceptual sizing and performance analysis across Advanced Air Mobility configurations and battery, hydrogen fuel cell, internal combustion engine, and hybrid energy systems.

My goal is to make design technology a partner in decision-making: supporting experts, accelerating innovation, and enabling safer, more sustainable mobility. I care about tools and knowledge that serve the community and contribute to human well-being.

01

Generative design
& dimensionality reduction

Reformulating feasible design domains with generative AI. VAEs trained on airfoil and wing shapes compress hundreds of geometric variables into a latent space of 10–20 variables while preserving performance accuracy.

An integrated aircraft design environment bridging propulsion, aerodynamics, and structural weight. CADE supports concept development for Regional Air Mobility, Urban Air Mobility, and unmanned aerial vehicles.
