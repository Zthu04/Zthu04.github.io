About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Aerospace and Mobility Engineering at Konkuk University, Seoul. My work is dedicated to integrating Scientific Machine Learning into the industrial design of aerospace systems.

My research specifically addresses large-scale aerodynamic shape optimization challenges, where massive design points and high-dimensional spaces often hinder traditional methods. I leverage Deep Generative Models (VAE) and Probabilistic Surrogates (MDN, BNN) to reduce design dimensionality and quantify performance uncertainties.

As the lead developer of the Comprehensive Aircraft Design Environment (CADE), I focus on the conceptual sizing and performance analysis of next-generation Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) and hybrid-electric aircraft.

My Research

1. Dimensionality Reduction in Design Spaces

My work reformulates feasible design domains using Generative AI. By training Variational Autoencoders on thousands of airfoil and wing shapes, we can compress hundreds of geometric variables into a latent space of just 10-20 variables without losing performance accuracy.

Visualizing the compressed design space for subsonic airfoil generation.

2. CADE: Multidisciplinary Design Optimization

I developed the CADE framework to bridge the gap between propulsion, aerodynamics, and structural weight. This is currently used for the concept development of Regional Air Mobility (RAM) aircraft.

Integrated propulsion-aerodynamics workflow within the CADE environment.
